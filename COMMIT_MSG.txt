Fix login: resolve MissingCSRF error and add email/phone login

Root cause: A custom CSRF route at /api/auth/csrf was overriding
NextAuth v5's built-in CSRF endpoint, setting a cookie named
"csrf_token" instead of "next-auth.csrf-token". NextAuth could not
find its own CSRF cookie during credential validation, causing
MissingCSRF errors on every login attempt.

Fixes:
- Move custom CSRF route from /api/auth/csrf to /api/csrf so
  NextAuth's built-in CSRF handler works at /api/auth/csrf
- Update CSRFProvider to fetch from /api/csrf
- Add trustHost: true and explicit cookie config to NextAuth
- Add AUTH_URL env var (preferred over NEXTAUTH_URL in v5)
- Rename credentials field from "phone" to "identifier" to accept
  either email or phone number
- Update authorize() to detect email (contains @) and look up by
  email, otherwise normalize phone and look up by phone
- Update login form label, placeholder, and Zod schema
